AI Entertainment Decision System

Two models.

Tell Optio what you feel like and two different recommenders answer at the same time — gradient boosting and a neural network. You pick whichever read you better, and that choice trains the system.

  • Two engines answering side by side, every time
  • Your account remembers what you liked and what you rejected
