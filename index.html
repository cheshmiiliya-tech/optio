    Welcome to

    Optio

    AI Entertainment Decision System

    1. Tell Optio about yourself. Six short questions — your name, what you like, who you're going with, where you are, and a favourite colour.
    2. Ask for something. "A funny film for tonight", "a co-op game", "live music this weekend".
    3. Two models answer at once. One is gradient boosting, the other a neural network. They often disagree.
    4. You pick the better shortlist. That choice is saved to your account and shapes what you're shown next time.
